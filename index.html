The story behind the game

Inspired by a real journey

Sometimes a move across Europe becomes something else entirely. A couple, a cat, a van full of boxes, and a week-long drive from Barcelona to Vilnius and back — through frozen pipes, golden hour detours, and the kind of silence that only empty highways at dawn can hold.

Road Home was born somewhere on that road. In the small rituals that kept the trip alive — her taking over the playlist, the first stop at a supermarket where nothing on the shelves made sense, chasing the last light for one more drone shot before dark. It's a game about the quiet magic of getting somewhere together, and the strange relief of finally making it home.
