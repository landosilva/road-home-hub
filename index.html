The story behind the game

Inspired by a real journey

Sometimes you meet someone who supports all your craziness and changes your life forever. What started as a joke — "I'll get a van and bring all your stuff here" — became reality once our connection grew deep enough to take the leap: drive 7,000 km together. Scary, a little crazy — and an adventure we couldn't say no to.

Road Home was born somewhere on that road. In the small rituals that kept the spirit alive — choosing the next song, stopping at remote gas stations where nobody speaks your language, stumbling onto views that make you pull over and just stand there, chasing the last light before dark and being up again before sunrise.

The trip demanded everything from us — constant collaboration, patience, trust. And somewhere between the frozen pipes and the golden sunsets, we felt it — every challenge pulling us closer together.

This game is my attempt to bottle that feeling — the joy of figuring things out together, the thrill of not knowing what's around the next corner, and the moment you realize the person next to you is the whole reason the trip matters.
