The story behind the game

Inspired by a real journey

Road Home is based on a true story: a couple who drove a brand new campervan from Barcelona to Vilnius and back — carrying boxes, a cat, a drone, and the kind of trust that only a thousand-mile detour can build. All within a week.

The game captures the full arc: buying bed sheets in a foreign supermarket, arguing about music, finding the perfect sunset spot for a drone shot, surviving a frozen water system in Lithuania, and the quiet relief of pulling into your street with everything — and everyone — intact.

Design pillars
❤

Cozy despite the danger

The van is warm. The road is uncertain. The contrast between home-on-wheels and the unknown is the soul of the game.

✨

A bond tested and strengthened

The journey is hard, but you come out the other side closer than before. The relationship is the real reward.

☀

The mundane is the magic

Buying kitchen gear in Poland, choosing a song for a mountain pass, pulling curtains for a daytime nap. Real moments make the best mechanics.
